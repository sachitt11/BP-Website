About Us

Small habits can make a big difference.

A Better Person Club is a high-school club where students can learn practical ways to take better care of themselves. We focus on healthy routines, personal hygiene, skincare, hair care, confidence, organization, and overall self-improvement.


Our goal isn't to make everyone look the same. Everyone is different, and there is no single "perfect" appearance. Instead, we help students build healthy habits that make them feel comfortable, confident, and prepared for everyday life.

What We Focus On

Learn useful habits that you can actually use in everyday life.

🧴

Skin Care

Learn simple, age-appropriate skincare habits such as gentle cleansing, moisturizing, and sun protection.

💇

Hair Care

Learn about hair hygiene, basic maintenance, different hair types, and finding routines that work for you.

🧼

Personal Hygiene

Build everyday routines involving showering, dental care, clean clothes, deodorant, and general hygiene.

🧠

Confidence

Work on confidence, communication, self-respect, and feeling comfortable being yourself.

👕

Personal Style

Explore ways to express yourself through clothing, organization, and personal style without comparing yourself to others.

🌱

Healthy Habits

Focus on sleep, hydration, cleanliness, organization, stress management, and other habits that support overall well-being.
